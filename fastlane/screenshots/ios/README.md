# App Store screenshots

The PNG files here are uploaded by `scripts/appstore.py`, in file-name order, one set per device, to the language of
their folder; App Store Connect shows the en-US ones in every language that has none of its own.

- **iPhone**, required: 2868x1320 (iPhone 16/17 Pro Max) or 2796x1290 (iPhone 15/16 Plus, 15/16 Pro Max), landscape or
  portrait, up to 10.
- **iPad**, required while the app runs on the iPad: 2752x2064 (iPad Pro 13" M4) or 2732x2048 (iPad Pro 12.9"), up to 10.

A file of another size is refused by name before anything is uploaded. Taken on the device with the side button and
volume up, sent to the X1, committed as they came.
