0.7.0: A new tuning curve with no borders: every octave moves key by key from the midrange's octave type to the bass's and the treble's, and the temperament octave is stretched too. Tune again from the start: the targets have changed. The key struck is caught sooner; the strobe's row stands at once, each band fading in, and you set how many bands it shows. Each register offers only the octave types its strings sound. Sampling needs eight strings besides A4.
