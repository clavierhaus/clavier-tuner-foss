New
- From C#6 up, two bars in the strobe's place. Strings there ring for about half a second, and a display that follows every moment flutters with the strike's attack and its fading tail. Now each strike gives one value, the string's pitch over its loud part, held on the bar until the next strike, with its cents and the change from the strike before. Turn the pin, strike again: the bar moves by what the pin did.
- The left bar is the key an octave below as tuned, white and fixed; the key's target is set from it by the treble's octave type. Help › Quick start › Above C6 explains the bars.
