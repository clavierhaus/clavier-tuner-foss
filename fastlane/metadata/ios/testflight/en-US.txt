0.9.0

New
- After sampling, the app suggests the bass's octave type from your sampled strings, with the reason behind the (i). One tap uses it; the choice stays yours. Sample two bass keys an octave apart (A1 and A2, say) to get one.

Changed
- From C6 up the strobe moves at the pin's pace: the shorter the string, the slower the bands, so a turn of the pin looks alike on every key. The left panel says so.
- The false-beat check has moved to Clavier Tuner Pro.
- No violet partials while tuning.
- A string a little off its target, struck after one further off, is read: the strobe no longer stays dark or comes and goes.
- A new phone starts on the six-point stretch. Three points stay one tap away in Settings › Stretch, and every octave type and width stays yours.

Please try
- Strike a string well off its target, then one nearer to it: does the strobe stay lit?
- Sample two bass keys an octave apart: does the suggested octave type match what you would choose?
