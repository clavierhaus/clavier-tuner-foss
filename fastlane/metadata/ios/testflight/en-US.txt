0.12.0

New
- A new way through a tuning. A4 and the sampling are on one start screen: the edge across the top (A4, Temperament, Octaves, the samples), the three steps under it, the whole keyboard at the foot with the next key to sample dotted and named. The tuning screen has the same edge (A4, Octaves, Strobe) and the keyboard at the foot with the keys tuned in green; Progress is gone.

Changed
- Settings as one list per tab: Tuning, Stretch, Piano, App, Advanced, Help, About.
- Help nested: a five-line Quick start with A4, the sampling, the octave types, the tuning screen and above C6 under it; then what each setting does.
- Done in the sampling sets the bass to the octave type your sampled strings suggest, and the tuning starts.

Please try
- A whole tuning through the new screens: A4, sampling, tuning. Does every step stay where you expect it, and does a tap on an earlier step take you back?
