# Privacy policy — Clavier Tuner

Clavier Tuner, by Clavierhaus GmbH, Vienna, Austria. In force from 23 November 2026, for the app on the iPhone and the iPad.

**The app collects no data.** Nothing you do in it leaves your device.

- **The microphone.** The app listens while you tune, to measure the string you strike. The sound is read on your device
  as it arrives and is gone with the next moment: it is not recorded, not stored and not sent anywhere.
- **What is kept on your device.** Your settings, and the tuning you are working on (the pitch of A4, the strings
  measured, the notes tuned), so that you can continue tomorrow. The tuning is kept in one file sealed with a key held by
  your device's keychain. Deleting the app deletes both.
- **No network.** The app makes no connection to the internet: no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, no
  tracking, no third-party code that does any of these.
- **Children.** The app collects nothing from anyone, children included.

The app is open source: anyone can read how it does what it says here, at
https://github.com/clavierhaus/clavier-tuner-foss.

Questions about this policy: Clavierhaus GmbH, Vienna, Austria, https://clavierhaus.at.
