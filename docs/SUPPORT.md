# Support — Clavier Tuner

Clavier Tuner is a piano tuning app by Clavierhaus GmbH, Vienna, Austria.

- **How it works:** Settings › Help in the app: a Quick start from A4 to the first note, and a topic on each step and
  each setting.
- **A question, a fault, a wish:** open an issue at https://github.com/clavierhaus/clavier-tuner-foss/issues. Say which
  device, which version (Settings › About), and what the piano and the screen were doing.
- **Privacy:** [docs/PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md). The app collects no data.
- **The code:** the whole app, its engine, its tests and the documents it is judged by, at
  https://github.com/clavierhaus/clavier-tuner-foss.

Clavierhaus GmbH, Vienna, Austria, https://clavierhaus.at.
